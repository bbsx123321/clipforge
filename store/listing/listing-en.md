## Full description

ClipForge is a lightweight Windows app that combines screen recording and video editing — from capture to final MP4 in a single window.

RECORDING
- Three capture modes: fullscreen, custom region, or a single app window
- Live red frame marks the selected region — what you see is what you record
- System audio and microphone captured together and mixed into the video
- Hardware accelerated encoding (NVIDIA / AMD / Intel, with automatic software fallback)
- Pause/resume, segmented recording and crash recovery keep your footage safe
- 3-second countdown, global hotkeys (default F10 start/stop, F11 pause/resume, customizable)
- Tray-friendly, multi-monitor support, English / Chinese interface

EDITING
- Built-in player: seek by slider, adjustable playback speed
- Timeline editing: pick start/end points, fine-tune by dragging the markers
- Speed up a selection (1.5x–4x), delete segments, multi-step undo
- One-click MP4 export with lossless trimming

LIBRARY
- Recent recordings list — double-click to play and edit
- Multi-select to delete files or remove entries

ClipForge collects no personal data, requires no network access, and stores recordings only on your PC.
