English:

ClipForge is a screen recorder with a built-in lightweight video editor. No sign-in, no account, no network required — all features work offline.

How to test:
1. Launch the app. A small floating control bar appears (this is the main window).
2. Click the red "Start" button (or press F10). A 3-second countdown shows, then recording starts and the app minimizes to the system tray (tray icon turns red).
3. Press F10 again (or click "Stop") to finish. The recording is saved as MP4 and appears in the "Recent" list below the control bar.
4. Double-click a recording in the list to open the built-in player & editor: use the Trim button to mark start/end points on the timeline, then "Speed up", "Delete selection", "Undo", and "Export MP4".
5. Global hotkeys: F10 = start/stop recording, F11 = pause/resume (customizable in Settings).

Notes:
- The window close button minimizes to tray (by design — tray-resident app). To fully quit: right-click the tray icon and choose "Quit".
- The gear icon opens Settings: capture mode (fullscreen / region / app window), system audio & microphone toggles, output folder, hotkeys, and UI language (Chinese / English).
- Recordings are saved only to the local output folder shown in Settings. The app collects no personal data (see privacy policy).

中文：

剪录坊 ClipForge 是一款录屏 + 轻量视频剪辑工具。无需登录、无账号、无网络要求，全部功能离线可用。

测试步骤：
1. 启动应用，出现悬浮控制条（即主窗口）。
2. 点击红色「开始」按钮（或按 F10），出现 3 秒倒计时后自动开始录制，同时应用最小化到系统托盘（托盘图标变红）。
3. 再按 F10（或点「停止」）结束录制，自动保存为 MP4 并出现在控制条下方的「最近录制」列表。
4. 双击列表中的录制条目，进入内置播放器与剪辑器：点「裁剪」在时间轴上标记开始/结束点，可使用「加速选区」「删除选区」「撤销」「导出 MP4」。
5. 全局快捷键：F10 = 开始/停止录制，F11 = 暂停/继续（可在设置中自定义）。

说明：
- 窗口关闭按钮为最小化到托盘（托盘常驻设计）。完全退出方式：右键托盘图标 → 「退出」。
- 齿轮图标打开设置：录制模式（全屏/区域/软件窗口）、系统声音与麦克风开关、输出目录、快捷键、界面语言（中文/English）。
- 录制内容仅保存在设置中显示的本地输出目录。应用不收集任何个人数据（见隐私政策）。
